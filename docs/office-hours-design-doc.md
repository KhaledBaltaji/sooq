## Problem Statement

Politically opinionated men (18-39) across the MENA region have no way to put money behind their political convictions. Sports betting exists through offshore platforms and informal channels, but political prediction markets — who becomes PM, will a regime fall, will there be a military strike — don't exist for Arabic-speaking audiences. Polymarket covers MENA events but serves Western crypto traders, not the people with actual information edges on local politics.

## Demand Evidence

**Status: Hypothesis-driven, no hard demand evidence yet.** The founder believes this is needed based on:
- Sports betting is already big in Lebanon/MENA — the behavior of risking money on outcomes exists
- People would "kill for" the ability to bet on politicians — reported but not verified with specific users
- No existing product serves this niche (Polymarket is US/crypto-centric, nothing is Arabic-first)

**Risk:** This is a conviction bet. The founder has not yet talked to specific users, observed workaround behavior, or collected willingness-to-pay signals. The first priority post-launch is validating that people will actually deposit real money on political outcomes.

## Status Quo

People argue about politics in coffee shops, Telegram groups, and WhatsApp. They bet on sports through offshore platforms and informal channels. For political outcomes, the current solution is: nothing. No one is currently betting on politics in MENA in any organized way. This is either a massive opportunity (unserved demand) or a warning sign (no one wants this badly enough to hack together a workaround).

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**Target user:** Politically opinionated Lebanese man, 18-39, who follows regional politics closely, already bets on sports, and would bet on political outcomes if a platform existed.

**Narrowest wedge:** Lebanese political markets (PM tenure, elections, government formation) as the beachhead. Lebanon is culturally permissive enough for betting, has a regulatory gray area (no explicit ban on online gambling), and has constant political drama. Expand to MENA-wide markets (Iran, Syria, Iraq, Gulf states) once Lebanese traction is proven.

**Key insight:** Lebanon alone is too small for a massive startup. But MENA as a whole has constant political events — there's always a crisis somewhere. Lebanon is the beachhead, MENA is the market.

## Constraints

- **Regulatory gray area:** Lebanon has no explicit ban on online gambling but ISP-blocks gambling sites and has a Casino du Liban monopoly. Platform must be resilient to regulatory action via: primary .com domain + Cloudflare proxy, at least one mirror domain, in-app messaging to users if primary domain is blocked.
- **Crypto behind fintech UX:** Users see USD everywhere. USDT/USDC deposits via 3pay are a payment rail, not the product identity. No blockchain jargon, no wallet addresses.
- **Arabic + English:** RTL support required. Lebanese users expect Arabic UI.
- **Real money:** This is a real-money platform from day one. No play money, no simulation.
- **2-week MVP timeline (aggressive):** Per the technical brief. This refers to AI-assisted (CC) execution time; human-equivalent effort is 3-4 months. Dark mode is deferred to post-validation as a polish item.
- **KYC posture:** No KYC at MVP. Implement tier-based thresholds (e.g., $500+ cumulative withdrawal triggers ID verification) in a fast-follow. This is a conscious tradeoff for launch speed.
- **Build for Lebanon only:** Multi-region, multi-currency, and localization architecture for broader MENA expansion is NOT an MVP concern. Design for one market (Lebanon), one language pair (Arabic + English), one currency (USD).

## Recommended Approach

**Approach A — Full Platform.** The founder has a thorough technical brief (technical-brief-v2.md) with all decisions already made. With AI-assisted development, the marginal cost of building the complete platform vs. a stripped MVP is minimal. Crucially, the agent/commission system needs to be tested alongside demand — if the distribution thesis (agents recruiting bettors) is as important as the demand thesis, you can't validate one without the other.

**Wireframe:** Core screens (Markets Home, Place Bet, Wallet) sketched at `/tmp/gstack-sketch-1774279099.html`. Key design decisions:
- Fintech aesthetic (clean, minimal, no crypto jargon)
- Market cards show question + volume + bettor count + Yes/No prices
- Bet placement shows locked odds, explicit fee breakdown, potential payout
- Wallet shows USD balance with deposit/withdraw actions and transaction history

**Critical technical decisions from the brief:**
- Pool-based payout model with locked ratios at placement (Option B)
- 7% platform fee, scaling factor for pot overflow edge cases
- Same-side-only betting: a user who has bet YES on a market cannot bet NO on the same market (and vice versa). Prevents wash-trading and price manipulation.
- Dynamic max bet algorithm: max_bet = pool_total × 0.15
- Seed money for initial liquidity ($200 per side). Represented in the ledger as transaction type `seed`. Included in pool totals for ratio calculations (provides liquidity). At resolution, seed money on the losing side goes to the platform, seed money on the winning side is reclaimed by the platform. Seed money is never distributed to winners.
- Market lock mechanism: admin can lock a market (no new bets accepted) independently of resolution. When a political event's outcome becomes known (e.g., election results at 2am), admin locks the market immediately to prevent post-outcome betting, then formally resolves within 24 hours.
- Agent commission: 5% of referred users' bet amounts, credited after market resolution (not instantly — see concerns below)

**Resolved implementation decisions (from initial review):**
1. **Commission timing:** Commissions are held in escrow until market resolution. The brief's "credited instantly" is overridden — instant crediting creates liability on voided/refunded markets.
2. **Balance concurrency:** Use a ledger pattern — append-only `transactions` table, balance derived via `SUM()`. All balance mutations must occur inside Postgres transactions (not Supabase client-side updates). No direct `balance_usd` column mutation.
3. **Transaction/ledger table:** Required. Every balance change (bet, win, deposit, withdrawal, commission, refund) is an append-only ledger entry. Balance = `SUM(amount) WHERE user_id = X`.
4. **Fee application:** The 7% fee is deducted from the total pot before calculating payouts. Not per-bet. Formula: `available_pot = total_pot × 0.93`. Each winner's payout = `bet_amount × locked_ratio`, scaled if sum exceeds `available_pot`.
5. **Seed money:** Seed money does NOT participate in payouts — it provides initial liquidity only. If YES wins, the NO seed money goes to the platform (not distributed to winners). This keeps the payout math clean and prevents the platform from subsidizing winners.

**Payout formula (inline):**
- At bet placement: `locked_ratio = current_total_pool / current_pool_for_the_side_being_bet`. This ratio is computed and stored per-bet at placement time, independently of which side ultimately wins. Example: if YES pool = $250, NO pool = $200, a YES bet locks ratio = ($250 + $200) / $250 = 1.80x.
- At resolution: `available_pot = total_pot × 0.93` (after 7% fee, excluding seed money)
- For each winning bet: `individual_payout = bet_amount × locked_ratio`
- If `SUM(all individual_payouts) > available_pot`: apply scaling factor `sf = available_pot / SUM(all individual_payouts)`, then `actual_payout = individual_payout × sf`

**Deposit flow:**
- User initiates deposit from Wallet screen, enters USD amount.
- 3pay widget opens (embedded or redirect) — user pays in USDT/USDC. User sees USD amount only.
- 3pay sends webhook on confirmation. Balance credited only after webhook confirmation (not optimistic).
- Minimum deposit: $5. Expected confirmation: 1-5 minutes (blockchain confirmation time).
- Failure/timeout: if no webhook within 30 minutes, show "deposit pending" status. Admin can manually reconcile.
- Fallback if 3pay unavailable: user sends USDT to a platform wallet address, admin manually credits balance after verification.

**Auth edge cases:**
- OTP expiry: 5 minutes. Max 3 retry attempts per 15-minute window. After 3 failures, cooldown for 30 minutes.
- Unsupported number: support Lebanese (+961), and common MENA country codes. If OTP provider doesn't support a number, show error with support contact.

**Supabase implementation constraint:**
All balance-affecting operations MUST be implemented as Postgres functions called via `supabase.rpc()`. Required Postgres functions: `place_bet(user_id, market_id, side, amount)`, `resolve_market(market_id, outcome)`, `process_deposit(user_id, amount, tx_ref)`, `process_withdrawal(user_id, amount)`. These functions contain all transaction logic — no client-side balance mutations.

**Agent dashboard (MVP scope):**
Agents get a simple dashboard page showing: total referred users, pending commissions (in escrow), paid commissions, and a list of their referral link activity. No tiered commission UI at MVP — all agents get 5%. If demand signal is weak in the first 2 weeks, the agent system is the first feature to cut in scope reduction.

**Scope risk acknowledgment:** The agent/commission system is a conscious dual-thesis bet — testing distribution alongside demand. This adds meaningful backend complexity before demand is validated. If no users deposit in the first 2 weeks, agent infrastructure is the first thing cut.

**Fee structure (canonical):**

| Fee | Rate | Source | When |
|-----|------|--------|------|
| Platform fee | 7% of total pot | Deducted at resolution | Before payout calculation |
| Agent commission | 5% of referred user's bet amount | From platform revenue | After market resolution |
| Effective rake on referred bets | 7% platform + 5% commission | Combined | At resolution |

Note: Agent commission comes out of the platform's 7% take, not on top of it. Net platform revenue on referred bets = 2%.

**Resolution mechanism:**
- Admin-only resolution at MVP. Admin selects the outcome based on the stated resolution source (defined at market creation, e.g., "Official Lebanese government gazette" or "AP/Reuters confirmation").
- Disputed outcomes: Admin can void a market (all bets refunded minus no fee) if the resolution source is ambiguous. Voided markets return all bets to users.
- Resolution triggers payout calculation immediately. Payouts are credited to user balances within the same database transaction.

**Withdrawal flow:**
- Users request withdrawal from their USD balance.
- Withdrawal is settled via 3pay as USDT/USDC to the user's specified wallet address (or mobile money if 3pay supports it).
- Minimum withdrawal: $10. Processing time: 24-48 hours (manual admin approval at MVP).
- Fallback if 3pay is unavailable: manual USDT transfer with admin confirmation.

**Agent attribution:**
- Each agent gets a unique referral code (e.g., `?ref=AGENT123`).
- Attribution is permanent — once a user signs up via a referral code, all their future bets generate commission for that agent.
- Commission = 5% of the referred user's bet amount on each bet, held in escrow until market resolution. If market is voided, commission is also voided.
