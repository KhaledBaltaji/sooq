## Affected Pages/Routes
- `/` (Markets Home) — market listing, real-time pool updates, activity feed
- `/market/[id]` (Market Detail) — bet placement, locked ratio display, share card
- `/wallet` — balance display, deposit flow (3pay), withdrawal request
- `/portfolio` — active bets, historical bets, win/loss record
- `/referral` — agent dashboard, referral link, commission tracking
- `/leaderboard` — top predictors by accuracy and profit
- `/admin/*` — market creation, resolution, withdrawal queue, user management
- `/api/webhook/3pay` — deposit confirmation webhook
- `/api/og/[marketId]` — dynamic OG image for share cards

## Key Interactions to Verify
- Bet placement: select side → enter amount → see locked ratio + potential payout → confirm → balance deducted
- Deposit: enter amount → 3pay widget → wait for webhook → balance credited → first-deposit bonus applied
- Withdrawal: enter amount → see fee breakdown → confirm → enters admin queue
- Market resolution (admin): lock market → select outcome → payouts calculated and credited atomically
- Share card: share button → WhatsApp/Telegram → recipient sees OG preview → deep link to market
- Push notification: market resolves → winner gets push → taps → sees updated balance

## Edge Cases
- Bet placement when market is locked/resolved (should reject)
- Bet placement exceeding dynamic max bet (should show error with current max)
- Bet placement during last 30 minutes (should reject)
- Deposit with duplicate tx_ref (idempotent — no double credit)
- Withdrawal with insufficient balance (should reject, show balance)
- Withdrawal from new account within 24 hours (should reject with timer)
- Market resolution with empty side (should void, refund all)
- Market with < $100 non-seed bets after 48 hours (should auto-cancel)
- Concurrent bet placement + market resolution (race condition — bet should be rejected)
- Scaling factor triggers on resolution (payouts scaled proportionally)
- Deposit bonus on first deposit ($5 free, 2x wagering requirement)
- Wagering requirement check on withdrawal (bonus funds wagered 2x?)

## Critical Paths
- Deposit → bet → market resolves → payout credited → withdrawal (full money flow E2E)
- Agent referral: signup via referral link → deposit → bet → market resolves → commission credited to agent
- Payout invariants: total payouts + fee + seed reclaim = total pot (property-based tests)
- Webhook security: HMAC verification, idempotency, rate limiting
- Balance reconciliation: balance_usd cache = SUM(transactions) for all users
